Privacy Policy

Hoop Land Plus · Last updated November 16, 2026

Hoop Land Plus (hooplandplus.com) is a free, fan-made companion web app for the game Hoop Land. It runs in your browser. We built it to collect as little as possible: there are no accounts, no sign-ups and no ads.

Your save files stay with you

When you load a Hoop Land save or league file, it's read and edited on your device, in your browser. It isn't uploaded to us. The app keeps your settings and work in progress in your browser's own storage (local storage and IndexedDB) so you can pick up where you left off. You can clear it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Dropbox (optional)

If you choose Connect Dropbox, you sign in on Dropbox's own page and allow the app access. With that access the app only:

  • saves the files you export (saves and league files) to the app's own folder in your Dropbox (inside your Dropbox's Apps folder). It can't see or change anything else in your Dropbox;
  • keeps a small backup of your player traits in that folder, so they carry over between devices;
  • creates a share link for a file you export, when you ask for one, so you can send it to someone;
  • opens a Dropbox share link you paste in, to load that file.

The sign-in uses Dropbox's secure PKCE method. The access token is kept only in your browser; it's never sent to us. You can disconnect in the app's settings at any time, and remove the app's access from your Dropbox account settings (Connected apps).

Usage counts

On hooplandplus.com we count page views and which features get used (for example "opened the roadmap") with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly counter. It uses no cookies and doesn't track you across sites. What it sees is the page or feature name, your screen size and the site you came from; it doesn't get your save files, names or anything you type. We use it only to see what people use and what to improve.

Bug reports

The Report a Bug button only copies a short description (app version, device type, which screen) to your clipboard so you can paste it in our Discord yourself. Nothing is sent automatically.

Other services

The app loads fonts from Google Fonts and team logos from public image links; those services see a normal web request from your browser. Season files for Start a Franchise come from this site. Links to Discord open Discord, which has its own privacy policy.

Children

Hoop Land Plus doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
